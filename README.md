## About

I am a Computer Science undergraduate focused on building strong fundamentals in programming, problem solving, and core computer science concepts. Alongside my BSCS studies, I am actively learning through hands-on practice and projects to prepare myself for real world engineering environments.

I take a disciplined and intentional approach to growth. Rather than chasing trends, I prioritize understanding fundamentals deeply and applying them in practical scenarios. I am motivated by progress that is measurable — skills built, problems solved, and systems understood.

At this stage of my journey, I am open to internship and learning opportunities that expose me to real development workflows, mentorship, and responsibility. I am especially interested in environments where I can learn fast, contribute meaningfully, and refine my technical direction through experience.

Outside of academics and technology, I am an avid esports enthusiast, enjoy playing badminton, and have a strong interest in traveling. These interests help me stay competitive, disciplined, and balanced — traits I carry into my professional work as well.

In the long term, my goal is to become a reliable and high impact software professional — someone who combines technical competence with consistency, problem solving ability, and leadership. I believe strong careers are built through sustained effort, curiosity, and ownership of one's growth.
